BROKER & PLATFORM BOOTCAMP

Deriv account setup guide

Learn the account process before funding anything. Always follow the current instructions shown in your own Deriv dashboard and check product availability in your country.

STEP 01

Create your Deriv profile

Use the official Deriv website, register with an email you control and complete the email confirmation. Check that you are on the genuine domain before entering details.

Official signup
STEP 02

Start with a demo account

Choose the demo/practice environment and use virtual funds. Practise navigating the platform and testing your plan without depositing real money.

Practice first
STEP 03

Complete verification if requested

Follow the secure in-account verification flow. Requirements can vary by country and account status. Never send identity documents or passwords to people in Telegram.

Account security
STEP 04

Add a Deriv MT5 account

From the account dashboard/Trader's Hub, choose the available MT5 account type. Review its product access, trading conditions and account currency before creating it.

MT5 account
STEP 05

Set MT5 login details

Create the MT5 password as instructed. Note the MT5 login ID and exact server shown in your Deriv dashboard. These can differ from your main Deriv email login.

Keep credentials private
STEP 06

Connect MT5

Install MT5 from the official MetaTrader source or your device's official app store. Select the matching Deriv server, enter the MT5 login ID and password, then confirm connection.

Platform connection
STEP 07

Find instruments and practise

Open Market Watch, search for an available symbol, open its chart and select a timeframe. Check the contract specifications before placing even a demo order.

Chart navigation
STEP 08

Understand deposits & withdrawals

Read the payment options, fees, limits, processing times and identity requirements displayed in your own account. Methods differ by location and can change.

No assumed payment method
STEP 09

Learn the real-account risks

Do not move to a real account because of one winning demo trade. Understand leverage, margin, spreads, commissions, swaps, slippage and the possibility of losing money.

Risk before funding

MARKET HISTORY & PEOPLE

Study established traders

Learn about different careers and schools of thought. These are historical case studies, not endorsements, copy-trade recommendations or proof that any method will work for you.

Jesse Livermore

Speculation · market behaviour

Study his historical observations about price action, market timing, patience and the dangers of oversized positions.

George Soros

Global macro

Explore macroeconomic themes, reflexivity and how expectations can influence market prices.

Paul Tudor Jones

Macro · risk discipline

Study his emphasis on protecting capital, adapting to market conditions and controlling downside risk.

Ed Seykota

Systematic trend following

Explore rules-based systems, trend following, position sizing and the role of trader psychology.

Richard Dennis

Trend following · Turtle experiment

Learn about systematic trading rules and the famous experiment that taught selected students a rule-based approach.

Linda Bradford Raschke

Short-term trading

Study professional process, technical setups, discipline and the importance of a repeatable routine.

Mark Minervini

Growth stocks · momentum

Explore stock selection, relative strength, trend templates and risk control in equity trading.

Warren Buffett

Long-term investing

Contrast investing with short-term trading: business value, patience, capital allocation and long holding periods.

A trader's public story is not a complete performance record. Famous examples can include survivorship bias: we hear more about people who became well known than those who tried similar approaches and failed.

Frequently asked questions

Is the TradeByJay course really free?

Yes. The educational material on this website is offered free. Third-party brokers, platforms, data services or payment providers may have their own charges and conditions.

Do I need a broker before starting?

No. Start with the lessons. When you reach the platform practice section, you can open a demo account with a provider available in your country.

Does TradeByJay guarantee profits?

No. No course, strategy, indicator or trader can guarantee a profit. Trading involves the risk of losing money.

Can I start with Deriv?

The site includes a Deriv setup learning guide and official help links. Check that the products and account features you want are available and permitted where you live.
