TRADEBYJAY SIGNATURE COURSE

APA strategy classroom

Learn the full Analysis · Patience · Action framework in order. This is an educational framework, not a live signal service or a promise of profitable trades.

H4 · MACRO BIAS

1. Establish direction

Read the broader swing structure. Define whether the market is bullish, bearish or unclear. Prioritise trades aligned with the H4 view; if direction is unclear, stand aside.

H1 · STRUCTURE & LIQUIDITY

2. Mark the important area

Map meaningful support/resistance, supply/demand, prior highs/lows and potential liquidity pools. Treat these as areas of interest—not guaranteed turning points.

M15 · SETUP CONFIRMATION

3. Wait for the setup

At the planned H1 area, look for the specific setup being tested: a liquidity sweep with return, a clear rejection, or a confirmed break-and-retest. Define candle-close requirements before testing.

M5 / M1 · EXECUTION

4. Refine the entry

Use lower-timeframe structure or momentum shift to refine an entry only after the M15 setup exists. Plan entry, invalidation, stop, target and position size before execution.

APA no-trade rule: No clear H4 direction, no meaningful H1 area, no M15 confirmation, or no sensible invalidation means no trade. Do not force a lower-timeframe trigger.

BEYOND THE CHART

Psychology, safety & funded trading

Build sound habits and understand the risks around online trading and funded-account programmes.

TRADING PSYCHOLOGY

Control the process

Use a pre-trade routine, define a maximum loss, take breaks after emotional decisions and review rule-following separately from profit or loss. Avoid revenge trading, FOMO, overconfidence and increasing size to recover.

SCAM AWARENESS

Recognise red flags

Be wary of guaranteed returns, “risk-free” signals, pressure to deposit quickly, fake screenshots, impersonated brokers, remote-access requests, withdrawal unlock fees and anyone asking for passwords or one-time codes. Verify firms through the relevant regulator's official register.

FUNDED TRADING

Understand challenge rules

Read the firm's legal entity, fee/refund terms, profit target, daily and total drawdown calculation, trailing rules, minimum days, news/overnight restrictions, prohibited strategies, payout conditions and termination clauses. A simulated funded challenge is not the same as a guaranteed funded brokerage account.

BACKTESTING

Test without fooling yourself

Write rules before reviewing charts, use chronological data, include spreads and fees, avoid look-ahead bias, record losing periods and validate on unseen data. Demo or historical performance does not guarantee live results.
