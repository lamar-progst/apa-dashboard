◈ TRADESBYJAY / LEARN THE SETUP

APA strategy, made simple.

A top-down process: use the bigger chart to understand direction, find an important price area, wait for a setup, then use the smaller chart to time the entry.

BEGINNER PLAYBOOK
H4 · THE COMPASS

Macro direction

Decide which side of the market you want to trade.

  • Bullish structure → look for BUYs
  • Bearish structure → look for SELLs
  • Use the swing structure and trend context
PURPOSE: DIRECTION
H1 · THE MAP

Key price zones

Mark where price may react. You are looking for location, not an entry yet.

  • Support / resistance
  • Supply / demand
  • Liquidity pools and swing highs/lows
PURPOSE: LOCATION
M15 · THE SETUP

Wait for confirmation

Watch how price behaves when it reaches an H1 area.

  • Liquidity sweep
  • Rejection candle
  • Break and retest
PURPOSE: CONFIRMATION
M5 / M1 · THE TRIGGER

Time the entry

Once the M15 setup appears, zoom in and wait for a clear trigger.

  • Momentum shift
  • Market Structure Shift (MSS)
  • Small structure break
PURPOSE: ENTRY TIMING

Example · A potential BUY setup

H4 bullish→H1 demand zone→M15 sweep / rejection→M5 or M1 bullish trigger

Only consider the BUY after the sequence develops. The reverse logic applies to a SELL in a bearish H4 direction.

Before you enter

  • ✓ H4 direction is clear
  • ✓ Price is at an H1 key area
  • ✓ M15 setup has formed
  • ✓ M5/M1 trigger is present
  • ✓ Stop loss has a clear invalidation point
  • ✓ Take profit has a logical target
Remember: H4 gives direction · H1 gives location · M15 gives setup confirmation · M5/M1 gives entry timing. A sweep is one possible M15 confirmation, not the only one. Wait for your actual trigger and manage risk; no setup is guaranteed.
